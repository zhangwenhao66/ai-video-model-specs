Use it

curl -s https://zhangwenhao66.github.io/ai-video-model-specs/models.json | jq '.models[] | select(.status=="available") | .model'

Notes

  • Clip length is per generation, before any extend or stitch feature.
  • "Up to 4K (upscaled)" means the model renders lower and the product upscales; it is not native 4K.
  • Sora 2 is kept for reference because many tutorials still point to it; OpenAI discontinued it in April 2026.
  • Veo 3.1 is being replaced by Gemini Omni in Google's apps; check the retirement date before starting a project on it.

The full, task-by-task index (text to video, image to video, upscalers, face swap, lip sync and more, 50 listings) is at AI Video Directory. Free options are compared on Free AI video tools.
